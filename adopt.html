How to Adopt a Pet

Follow these easy steps to bring your new best friend home!

Browse Pets

Explore a wide variety of pets available for adoption. Find the one that matches your lifestyle.

Fill Out Adoption Form

Complete an adoption application to provide us with your information and preferences.

Meet your potential pet and spend time together to see if it’s the right match.

Adopt & Bring Them Home

Once approved, complete the adoption process and take your new friend home!

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I adopt a pet?

To adopt a pet, browse our listings, click "Adopt Me," and submit an application. Our team will review your details.

What is the adoption fee?

The adoption fee covers care, food, and medical expenses. Fee details are available on each pet's profile.

Can I adopt a pet if I live in an apartment?

Yes, as long as the pet's needs are met. We assess compatibility before approval.

How long does the adoption process take?

It typically takes 1-2 weeks. We ensure a smooth process for both adopters and pets.

Can I return a pet after adoption?

We encourage thoughtful adoption, but if needed, contact us to arrange a safe return.

Are you Really Wroking 365 Days?

Yes,the animals need care every day! We work and need volunteer help every day of the year, even major holidays and even in inclement weather.
