About The PetNest

The PetNest for Adoption is a non-profit, 501-C3 organization dedicated to helping homeless animals in our community find permanent, loving homes where they will be treated as family members for the rest of their lives.

Our Mission

We strive to rescue and rehabilitate animals in need, ensuring they receive medical care, nourishment, and love. Our mission is to advocate for animal welfare, educate communities, and promote responsible pet ownership.

With so many dogs in need, how do we choose?

Our dedicated volunteers and staff carefully select pets based on their ability to adapt to loving homes, ensuring each one gets the second chance they deserve.
